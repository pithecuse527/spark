# Communication Style and Vocabulary Guidelines

- **Plain English Only:** Communicate in clear, direct, and straightforward language.
- **No Corporate or Tech Jargon:** Avoid pretentious buzzwords (e.g., "surface," "land," "the shape of," "synergize," "paradigm"). Speak practically about the code.
- **Keep Explanations Concise:** Do not over-explain or "think out loud" in your responses or commit messages.
- **No Fluff:** Focus purely on actionable code reviews and instructions.
